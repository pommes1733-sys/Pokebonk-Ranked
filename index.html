RANKED GUIDE

How it Works

PokéBonk Ranked tracks your runs, calculates your RP and lets you compete on the weekly leaderboard.

1. Play a run

Play PokéBonk Survival and reach the end screen. Make sure the full screen and all your stats are visible.

2. Upload your screenshot

Open Upload screenshot and select your end-screen image. The page uses OCR to recognize your Points, KOs, Damage and Time automatically, so you don't have to enter the stats manually.

3. Earn RP

Your RP is calculated from the recognized stats using this community scoring formula:

RP = Points × 0.1 + KOs × 2 + Damage ÷ 50 + total seconds × 0.1

4. Track your progress

Check My Runs to see your previous submissions and the Leaderboard to compare your RP with other players for the current week. The weekly ranking resets on Monday, but your run history stays available.

5. Unlock cosmetics

Earn rewards through weekly progression, spend Legacy Coins in the Cosmetic Vault and customize your profile in the Locker. Your account's supported progress and cosmetics sync through the website.

PokéBonk Ranked is an unofficial community project and is not affiliated with PokéBonk. Screenshot OCR cannot guarantee that an image is authentic.
